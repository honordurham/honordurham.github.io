Hi! My name is Honor Durham, and I am a Research Support Associate at the [MIT Election Data + Science Lab](https://electionlab.mit.edu/). I graduated from the University of Wisconsin-Madison in May 2025 with degrees in political science and data science. My research interests lie in American and comparative politics with a primary focus on elections. I enjoy researching and learning about American elections (election administration and voter behavior), democratic backsliding in modern democracies, and British politics. Outside of political science, I am passionate about podcasting, statistics, and R/Python. I plan to apply for graduate school for Fall 2027.
